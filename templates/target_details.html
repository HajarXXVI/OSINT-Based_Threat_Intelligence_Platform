Passive OSINT — No Exposed Network Signals Detected

A low passive score is not proof the target is secure.

ThreatLens performs passive intelligence: it reads public records from Shodan, NVD, and VirusTotal without ever contacting the target. It therefore assesses network exposure and reputation.

This scan found no exposed ports and no known CVEs. That means either the host is genuinely hardened, or it sits behind a service such as a CDN/WAF that hides its true footprint, or its weaknesses live in the web application layer (e.g. SQL injection, XSS) — flaws that only active crawling reveals and that passive OSINT cannot see.

For a complete verdict on a live web application, an active scanner would be required — which is deliberately outside ThreatLens's no-contact design.
